# Memobloom | 时刻繁花

**AI software for understanding, finding, and shaping visual memory.**

Memobloom is an open-source organization building local-first AI tools for photographers, filmmakers, content teams, and multimodal developers. Our projects focus on photo and video asset management, semantic search, video understanding, highlight discovery, editing assistance, delivery workflows, and on-device multimodal intelligence.

Our goal is simple: help creators stop drowning in raw material and start working with an AI workspace that understands assets, scenes, context, and delivery needs.

## What We Believe

Visual work is not just file management. It is a full chain of importing, analyzing, searching, selecting, editing, reviewing, and delivering.

Memobloom exists to make that chain smarter, safer, and more explainable:

- **Local-first by design**: photos, videos, client materials, indexes, and AI analysis results should stay on infrastructure you control by default.
- **Original assets stay untouched**: AI can understand and organize media without rewriting source files.
- **Natural language as the interface**: find moments like "parents applauding at the ceremony," "slow-motion running at sunset," or "high-emotion clips for an opening sequence."
- **Traceable decisions**: every search, recommendation, selection, export, and editing suggestion should have evidence behind it.
- **Creators remain in control**: AI should reduce repetitive work, while photographers, editors, and creative teams keep final judgment.

## Project Ecosystem

| Project | Focus | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **MemoBrain** | Local-first AI workflow infrastructure for visual assets | Provides ingestion, analysis, indexing, retrieval, agent workflows, trace records, and delivery foundations for photo and video libraries. |
| **MemoClip** | AI-assisted culling, editing, and delivery workspace | Helps creators find delivery-ready clips, selected photos, highlight moments, and story structures from large media collections. |
| **MemoChat** | Natural-language agent for media libraries | Lets users search, explain, organize, propose, and review visual projects through a conversational assistant. |
| **MemoRAG** | Multimodal retrieval and evidence system | Turns EXIF, captions, tags, visual descriptions, quality scores, faces, scenes, and similarity groups into searchable evidence. |
| **MemoVelo** | On-device video multimodal understanding engine | Focuses on local video understanding, highlight detection, segment localization, visual semantics, and lightweight multimodal inference. |

Together, these projects are becoming a complete **AI visual memory system** for independent creators, studio teams, event production, wedding delivery, commercial shoots, sports media, short-form video pipelines, and local multimodal applications.

## Use Cases

### Photographers and Retouching Teams

- Find key people, scenes, expressions, and moments across thousands of wedding, event, conference, portrait, or commercial photos.
- Detect blur, closed eyes, burst duplicates, similar compositions, and high-priority delivery candidates.
- Build candidate sets, delivery sets, and quality review reports based on client requirements.

### Video Editors and Content Teams

- Locate clips by intent: opening atmosphere, emotional peak, audience reaction, product close-up, motion highlight, or transition material.
- Break long footage into searchable, reusable, and explainable visual segments.
- Generate highlight suggestions and rough story structures for shorts, campaign videos, recaps, and event reels.

### Local AI and Multimodal Developers

- Build image/video understanding, retrieval, RAG, and agent workflows that can run locally.
- Explore lightweight models on real-world media libraries.
- Combine multimodal analysis, vector search, databases, task queues, and UI workflows into usable products.

## Technical Direction

Memobloom is built around local-first multimodal intelligence and traceable agent workflows:

- **Multimodal understanding**: image quality, scene recognition, face clustering, similarity grouping, video segment understanding, and fusion across visual, subtitle, audio, and metadata signals.
- **Semantic retrieval and RAG**: structured filters, full-text search, vector retrieval, multi-index fusion, reranking, constraint verification, and evidence packs.
- **Agent workflows**: natural-language planning, tool calls, human confirmation, audit logs, proposals, and delivery actions.
- **Local runtime**: macOS-first development, on-device models, optional local databases, and CLI/WebUI/desktop app surfaces.
- **Reusable infrastructure**: FastAPI, LangGraph, Qwen-Agent, PostgreSQL/pgvector, Valkey/Arq, Next.js, MLX, Core ML, and related local AI tooling.

## Why "Memobloom"

**Memo** means memory, context, and the story behind every asset.

**Bloom** means the moment when hidden material becomes visible, organized, and ready to be told.

In visual creation, the most valuable thing is rarely a single file. It is the emotion, relationship, action, and narrative value captured in a specific moment. Memobloom helps those moments emerge from massive media collections and become searchable, usable, and deliverable work.
